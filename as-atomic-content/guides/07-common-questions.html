Common questions

Why is my Card not visible?

Check that the Card is published and selected in the block. In AlphaBlock, confirm that the query returns published posts and Show Posts is set to Card. In AlphaCard, confirm that the Card row has a selected published Card and that needed token values are filled in.

Will changing a Card update every use?

Changing its shared markup can affect every block using that Card. The content fed into each placement can still differ. Check representative uses after editing a widely used Card.

Why use a Row instead of a Pattern?

Use a Row when the site should keep one shared section and update its placements together. Use an unsynced Pattern when editors need a layout to copy and then tailor to each page.

Why did my new Pattern Category not save?

Typing the name alone does not assign it. In the Pattern editor, press Enter or comma after the name so it becomes a category chip, then click Save. You can also create categories in Settings > Pattern Categories and then assign them in a Pattern.

Why are some blocks or controls missing?

Cards and Rows depend on ACF for their registered fields and post types. AlphaBlock, AlphaCard, the Row block, and wrapper rendering are supplied by the active theme or separate components. Ask the site team to check the relevant component installation and permissions if a control is absent.
