Atomic Design — what is it?

Atomic design is a way to build a site from repeatable parts. Instead of adjusting the same layout in many places, the team defines a shared component and uses it wherever that design is needed. A change to the shared source can then improve every placement.

Why it matters on an existing site

Most editors arrive after a site has already been built. Before changing a repeated section, find out whether it comes from a Card, a Row, or a Pattern. Editing a shared source may affect many pages; editing a copy may affect only the current page. That difference determines what to review before saving.

Practical principles

  • Reuse a design with a purpose. Use an existing component when its structure and behaviour fit the content.
  • Keep the source clear. Name components for what they do so another editor can find them.
  • Check the reach of a change. Review representative pages when editing a shared component.
  • Respect local content. A page can still have unique copy and imagery inside an approved layout.

What “Atoms” means here

The Atoms menu brings together Card templates, reusable Rows, and WordPress Patterns. They all support consistency, but they differ in what is stored and whether later edits affect existing placements. The name describes the site's design system; it does not mean every paragraph needs to become a reusable component.
